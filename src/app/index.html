How BrickScan works

  1. Scan the object with a LiDAR phone app (Polycam, Scaniverse, 3D Scanner App) on a flat, plain surface, indoors, and export OBJ, PLY, USDZ or GLB. Geometry is used; colours and textures are ignored.
  2. Load the file. BrickScan removes the floor and background, drops noise, lines the object up with the stud grid and seals holes.
  3. Choose a size by height, width, longest side or a brick budget.
  4. The solid shape is converted into standard bricks with staggered joints, hollowed where nobody will see, and checked: every brick connected, supported from below, no overlaps.
  5. Export a BrickLink wanted-list XML, a Rebrickable CSV, an LDraw file for BrickLink Studio / LDCad, or printable step-by-step instructions.

Everything runs locally in this page, with a built-in parts catalogue. Prices are offline estimates — import your own price list for an accurate cart total.
